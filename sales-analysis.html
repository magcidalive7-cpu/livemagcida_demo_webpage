Sales Data Analysis

By Live Magcida

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover trends in revenue, profitability, and regional performance. The goal is to provide actionable business insights that can improve decision-making.

Objectives

  • Analyze sales performance over time
  • Identify most profitable product categories
  • Evaluate regional sales distribution
  • Understand relationship between sales and profit

Tools & Technologies

  • Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
  • Excel

Key Insights

  • Sales show consistent growth trends over time, indicating business expansion.
  • Some categories generate high sales but low profit, suggesting cost inefficiencies.
  • Regional disparities highlight opportunities for targeted market strategies.
  • Higher sales do not always translate to higher profit, emphasizing the importance of margin analysis.
