Economic Data Analysis

By Live Magcida

Project Overview

This project explores key macroeconomic indicators including inflation and unemployment. The goal is to identify trends, relationships, and insights that explain economic performance over time.

Objectives

  • Analyze inflation trends over time
  • Examine unemployment patterns
  • Identify correlations between economic variables

Tools & Technologies

  • Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
  • Excel
  • Jupyter Notebook

Key Insights

  • Inflation in South Africa shows cyclical patterns influenced by global and domestic shocks.
  • Unemployment demonstrates structural persistence, remaining high regardless of short-term inflation changes.
  • The relationship between inflation and unemployment does not strongly follow the traditional Phillips Curve, indicating deeper structural issues in the economy.
